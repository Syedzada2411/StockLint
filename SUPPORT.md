# Support StockLint

StockLint's immediate goal is to help small sellers catch inventory CSV errors without uploading their business data to a service. The working preview is available under the MIT license, and the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) outlines review, rules, and accessibility work that would make it more useful.

## Ways to help now

1. Try the fictional sample and report a reproducible bug. Never post customer inventory in a public issue.
2. Suggest a generic inventory validation rule and explain an example where it matters.
3. Contribute tests, documentation, translation review, or accessibility feedback.
4. Share the project with a seller or operations team who could test it with their own data locally.

## Funding interest

Business backers or grant programs can support defined roadmap milestones. Before accepting money, the maintainer should publish an eligible payment route, proposed budget, scope, and progress reporting cadence. There is no active donation link in this repository. Funding does not buy access to private customer data, hidden features, or a promise of platform-specific compatibility.

For project discussion, open a GitHub issue after publication. Do not send financial details through issues.
