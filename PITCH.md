# StockLint · project brief for potential backers

## The problem

Small sellers often move product catalogs between spreadsheets and online systems. A duplicate SKU, missing product name, or invalid stock/price field can be hard to spot in a long CSV. Uploading real inventory to an online checker also introduces a privacy tradeoff.

## What exists now

The MIT-licensed early preview runs locally in a browser. It maps four inventory columns, checks rows, logs its limited whitespace cleanup, and exports an audited CSV. The fictional demo makes the workflow easy to evaluate without any account or customer data.

## What support would enable

1. An explicit review and undo flow for proposed corrections.
2. Reusable rule presets, with tests and fictional example catalogs.
3. Accessibility testing, documentation, and reviewed translations.

These are proposed milestones, not completed features. The acceptance evidence is defined in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Public releases and changelog entries would let supporters check progress.

## Why support it

The code can be reused, audited, and hosted by any team. A local-first design makes it useful when a seller does not want to send inventory to a third party. Human review remains part of the process; the tool does not invent missing business values or claim compatibility with any vendor's import format.

## Ways to participate

Try the demo; share reproducible, synthetic examples; contribute a test or documentation; or discuss a scoped development grant or business sponsorship once an eligible payment route and budget are published. No financial return or guaranteed business outcome is offered.
